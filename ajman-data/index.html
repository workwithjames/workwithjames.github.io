How to interpret it

Live fetching, quarterly source updates.

The page checks the official data automatically, but Ajman's publisher updates these datasets quarterly. The records are grouped by project and property characteristics, rather than exposing individual buyer or seller details.

Government open data

The source is Ajman Data and the Department of Land and Real Estate Regulation, not a property portal or listing provider.

Aggregated records

Each row can combine several transactions sharing a project, district, unit type, room count, year and quarter.

Decision context

Market totals help with research, but a purchase or valuation still needs property-specific comparables and legal due diligence.

Official sources

Real Estate Units Sales and Real Estate Units Mortgage, Ajman Data. Both are published under CC BY 4.0 with a declared quarterly update frequency.

Questions

About the Ajman data page

Is this Ajman transaction data live?

The website retrieves the newest available government records automatically. The source itself updates quarterly, so it is a live connection to the latest published dataset, not a transaction-by-transaction feed.

Does the page show individual owners or buyers?

No. The official datasets provide aggregated project, district, unit-type, transaction-count and value information. They do not expose personal buyer or owner records.

Can this data be used as a property valuation?

No. It is a market-research screen. A valuation requires suitable unit-level comparables and adjustments for building, condition, floor, view, tenancy, service charges and other factors.
