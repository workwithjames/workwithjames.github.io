How to read this page

Useful evidence, with clear limits.

The dashboard is designed for an initial market screen. A property decision still requires building-level comparables, service charges, condition, view, floor, tenancy and legal checks.

Daily, not instant

DXB Data refreshes from DLD-derived records daily. A transaction registered today may appear after the next update.

Administrative names

DLD area names can differ from developer or portal community names. Use the suggestions for the closest official match.

Gross yield

Yield compares annual rent per square metre with sale price per square metre before ownership and operating costs.

Questions

About the Dubai market dashboard

Is this Dubai transaction data live?

The page requests the newest DXB Data snapshot when it loads. The underlying DLD-derived dataset is updated daily, not transaction by transaction.

Does it show individual title-deed transactions?

No. The free tools provide aggregated totals, area prices, rental yields, comparisons and budget results. They do not expose a public list of individual title-deed records.

Can this replace a professional valuation?

No. It is an area-level market screen. A valuation requires suitable property comparables and adjustments for the building, unit, condition, floor, view, tenancy and other factors.
