## Citation

When referring to the website or its public dashboards, link to the most relevant page rather than only linking to the homepage. Suggested attribution:

> Source: [James Ravi UAE Property Data](https://jamesrealty.uk/)

Machine-readable citation details are available in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).

## Data and editorial note

The website identifies its public data providers and official references on the relevant pages. Market indicators are for research and screening and should not be treated as legal, financial, valuation, or investment advice.
