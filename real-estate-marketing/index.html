Questions

Before discussing a property campaign

Can the first discussion start without account access?

Yes. The first step is a commercial and operational brief. Access is considered only after a relevant scope is agreed.

Can roadshows and international campaigns be included?

Yes. Include the event location, dates, target market, project, expected meetings, budget and attending sales team.

Can CRM follow-up be included with lead generation?

Yes. Lead routing, stage rules, reminders, broker notifications and conversion feedback can be considered as part of the journey.
