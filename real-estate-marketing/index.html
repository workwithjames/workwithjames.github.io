Real estate marketing questions

Questions about real estate marketing and lead generation in Dubai

What does real estate marketing in Dubai include?

A connected system can include positioning, campaign strategy, creative, paid media, landing pages, lead forms, WhatsApp journeys, qualification, CRM routing, follow-up automation, conversion tracking and performance reporting.

How can real estate lead quality be improved?

Align the offer, audience, creative, landing experience and qualification questions with the sales team's real requirements. Then return contactability, meeting and sale outcomes to campaign reporting.

Can CRM automation be included with real estate lead generation?

Yes. The journey can include assignment rules, response timelines, stage definitions, reminders, broker notifications, follow-up sequences and conversion feedback.

Can international property campaigns and roadshows be supported?

Yes. Include the project, target country, required languages, media budget, event dates, attending sales team and expected commercial outcome.
