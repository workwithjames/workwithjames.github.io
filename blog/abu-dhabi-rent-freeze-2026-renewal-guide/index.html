Abu Dhabi tenancy

Abu Dhabi rent freeze 2026: Can a landlord still increase the rent?

Abu Dhabi's temporary measure changed the annual rental increase from 5% to 0% until further notice. A renewal notice showing an increase should be checked against the registered contract and the property's legal jurisdiction.

By James RaviPublished 1 August 20267 min read

Quick answer

Can rent rise at renewal during the Abu Dhabi freeze?

ADREC changed the annual rental increase from 5% to 0% for the duration of a temporary measure announced on 3 June 2026 and stated that it will remain in place until further notice. For a covered tenancy renewing on the same registered basis, a 5% increase should be challenged and explained in writing.

Check the jurisdiction: do not rely on a headline alone. Confirm that the property and agreement fall under the ADREC measure, especially where a separate legal or financial-zone regime may apply.

What does the temporary 0% measure say?

The Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre announced that residential, commercial and industrial tenancy renewals would be processed at a 0% increase during the temporary measure. The previous annual cap was 5%.

ADREC also said lease renewals and new agreements would reference the rental rate in the property's last registered Tawtheeq contract. The practical purpose is to create a stable reference point while the measure remains active.

The three details to retain

  • The measure took effect immediately on 3 June 2026.
  • The permitted annual increase is 0% for its duration.
  • The official wording says it remains in place until further notice.

Which properties and contracts are covered?

The ADREC announcement refers to residential, commercial and industrial properties across Abu Dhabi. It is relevant to registered contracts and the Tawtheeq record used for renewal.

A specific case can still turn on the identity of the contracting parties, the registered rent, whether the agreement is genuinely a renewal, and the legal jurisdiction governing the property. If those points are unclear, ask ADREC rather than assuming that a standard renewal notice is correct.

What should a tenant do after receiving a 5% increase?

Start by asking the landlord or property manager why the increase was issued. Some notices are generated as part of a normal renewal workflow and may not have been updated after the 0% measure.

Reply in writing

  1. Attach the proposed renewal.
  2. Reference the 0% ADREC measure.
  3. State the last Tawtheeq rent.
  4. Ask for a corrected renewal or legal basis.
  5. Keep the full correspondence.

If it is not corrected

  1. Confirm the property jurisdiction.
  2. Contact ADREC through official channels.
  3. Request a complaint reference.
  4. Do not sign unclear terms under pressure.
  5. Consider the dispute process or legal advice.

A calm request for clarification is often enough. If management maintains the increase, it should be able to identify the legal basis and explain why the temporary cap does not apply to that tenancy.

What should landlords and property managers do?

Renewal templates, CRM reminders and rent-review workflows should be updated so they do not continue adding the previous 5% cap automatically. The registered Tawtheeq amount should be checked before terms are sent.

Separating rent from legitimate service, maintenance or administrative charges also reduces disputes. Each amount should have a clear contractual and regulatory basis.

What does the freeze mean for property decisions?

A temporary rent freeze affects short-term income assumptions, but it does not determine a property's full investment case. Buyers should model net income using the registered rent, realistic vacancy, service charges, maintenance and financing costs.

Market activity also needs context. Abu Dhabi recorded strong transaction and foreign-investment growth in the first half of 2026, covered in the Abu Dhabi H1 property data guide. High transaction value, however, is not a substitute for property-level due diligence.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Abu Dhabi rent increase limit in 2026?

ADREC announced a temporary 0% annual increase for residential, commercial and industrial tenancy renewals from 3 June 2026 until further notice, replacing the previous 5% cap for the duration of the measure.

Can a management company add 5% at renewal?

For a tenancy covered by the temporary measure, a 5% renewal increase appears inconsistent with the announced 0% cap. Ask for the legal basis in writing and compare the proposal with the last registered Tawtheeq contract.

What rent should be used for an Abu Dhabi renewal?

ADREC states that lease renewals and new agreements during the measure will reference the rental rate in the property's last registered Tawtheeq contract.

What can a tenant do if the increase is not withdrawn?

Keep the renewal notice and Tawtheeq record, contact ADREC through its official channels and consider the Abu Dhabi real estate dispute process or independent legal advice if the issue remains unresolved.
