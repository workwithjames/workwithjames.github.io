FAQ

Before you start.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Monthly management packages cover strategy, setup, optimisation and reporting. The advertising budget is paid separately to the platform.

Can I start with only one service?

Yes. Website development, paid advertising, SEO, social media, branding and automation can all be scoped separately.

Do you work with existing websites and ad accounts?

Yes. Existing setups can be audited first, then improved without rebuilding everything unnecessarily.

Are packages fixed?

The displayed packages provide a clear starting point. Final scope can be adjusted when the project requires different deliverables, platforms or production work.

How do website payments work?

For standard website projects, work can begin with a deposit and the balance can be tied to agreed delivery milestones in the proposal.
