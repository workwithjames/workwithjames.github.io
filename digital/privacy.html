Privacy

Clear handling of enquiry and website data.

This notice covers analytics and the secure project/call forms on James Digital.

Summary

Project and call requests are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database so James can follow up. The forms collect only the information shown on the page plus basic source/referrer metadata.

Privacy notice

Analytics

Google Tag Manager may load analytics or marketing measurement configured through that container. Those tools may collect device and interaction data according to their configuration.

Enquiries

When you submit a project or call request, the supplied name, company, email, optional phone, project context, budget/timeline where supplied, source URL and campaign attribution may be stored for business follow-up.

Purpose and retention

Enquiry information is used to assess fit, respond, prepare a proposal or manage a potential business relationship. It should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and legitimate record-keeping.

Contact

Privacy questions or data requests can be sent to james@jamesrealty.uk.
