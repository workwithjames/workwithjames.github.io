Retail journey

Make each campaign strengthen the customer relationship, not just the promotion.

Retail performance improves when the product, offer, channel, landing experience and retention plan are designed as one flow.

Discovery

Help customers find the right product faster

Search, social content, paid media and category architecture should reduce browsing effort rather than simply increase traffic.

Conversion

Match the landing journey to the campaign promise

Offer pages, product detail, trust, delivery information and calls to action need to continue the expectation created by the ad or content.

Omnichannel

Connect online interest with store behaviour

Where physical retail matters, local search, store information, messaging and campaign attribution should support the visit rather than compete with ecommerce.

Retention

Use first-party signals after purchase

Email, CRM, segmentation and repeat-purchase journeys should reduce dependence on reacquiring the same customer through paid media.
