Why James Digital

Experience from agency, developer and brokerage environments, applied to one business problem.

The strongest digital work rarely fails because one channel is missing. It usually fails because strategy, media, website conversion, CRM and sales feedback are being managed as separate activities.

James Digital was built around the opposite model. James stays close to the commercial objective, owns the senior decisions and brings specialist execution into the engagement only where it adds value.

The point is not to imitate a large agency. The point is to give a business direct senior ownership with enough specialist capacity to execute properly.

Direct senior access

Less account management. More accountable decision-making.

The delivery model is intentionally lean. Specialist support can expand, but senior ownership does not get delegated away.

Traditional layered model

More handoffs between the business problem and the work.

  1. Business brief
  2. Sales or account layer
  3. Strategy handoff
  4. Production handoff
  5. Client receives the output
James Digital model

Senior ownership stays close to the commercial objective.

  1. Business problem
  2. James diagnoses and prioritises
  3. Specialists join where required
  4. James reviews decisions and delivery
  5. Performance feeds the next decision
