Solution / Lead Follow-up

Protect the demand you already paid to generate.

A lead is not a result if it waits in an inbox, reaches the wrong owner or disappears without a status. Follow-up needs visible ownership, timing, stages and feedback.

Discuss this problemCRM capability
Commercial outcome

Faster response, fewer lost enquiries and a CRM trail that shows what happens after marketing creates demand.

Where leads leak

The most expensive CRM problem is often operational.

Automation helps only after ownership and stage definitions are clear.

Speed

Response time is unknown

New enquiries enter multiple inboxes or systems without a visible service level or escalation route.

Routing

Ownership is inconsistent

Leads are manually assigned, duplicated or sent to people without the right project, market or product context.

Stages

Status labels do not describe reality

Teams use broad stages such as open or closed, so qualification and opportunity movement cannot be measured reliably.

Feedback

Marketing never sees sales outcomes

Paid channels optimise for submissions because qualified, meeting and closed outcomes are not returned to the decision loop.

Follow-up system

Make every enquiry visible from arrival to outcome.

01 / Intake

Lead-source and field design

Preserve source, campaign and qualification context at the point of capture instead of rebuilding it manually later.

02 / Routing

Ownership and SLA

Define assignment rules, response expectations, fallback owners and escalation for unattended enquiries.

03 / CRM

Commercial stage model

Use stages that describe contact, qualification, meeting, opportunity and outcome so pipeline movement is measurable.

04 / Automation

Nurture and feedback loops

Automate appropriate reminders and nurture while returning meaningful downstream outcomes to marketing decisions.

Evidence

CRM performance needs more than open rates.

Documented nurture performance can show message engagement, while stage movement and sales feedback show whether the system creates commercial value.
