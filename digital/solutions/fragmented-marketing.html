Solution / Fragmented Marketing

Make separate marketing activities operate as one commercial system.

Fragmentation happens when campaigns, content, website work, CRM, agencies and reporting all move independently. The fix is clearer ownership, shared priorities and one customer journey.

Discuss this problemStrategy capability
Commercial outcome

Fewer disconnected initiatives, clearer ownership and more reliable decisions across demand, conversion and follow-up.

Common symptoms

Activity is high, but accountability is blurred.

The issue is often operational rather than creative. Teams need a shared decision framework before adding more campaigns.

Ownership

Several suppliers own pieces, nobody owns the journey

Media, web, content and CRM work to separate briefs and success measures.

Priority

Every request becomes urgent

Teams lack a commercial roadmap, so campaigns and production compete for attention rather than sequence around impact.

Reporting

Dashboards disagree

Channel reports use different definitions, attribution windows and KPIs, making executive decisions harder.

Customer journey

The handoffs are invisible

Prospects move between ads, website, forms, CRM and sales without consistent context or ownership.

Operating model

Organise the work around decisions, owners and evidence.

01 / Priorities

Commercial roadmap

Define the business outcomes, major constraints, high-value workstreams and what should stop or wait.

02 / Ownership

Roles and supplier boundaries

Clarify who decides, who executes, who approves and who owns measurement across internal and external teams.

03 / Journey

Connected customer path

Align campaigns, landing pages, CRM stages and sales follow-up around the same audience and proposition.

04 / Governance

Review rhythm and scorecard

Create a practical cadence for decisions, blockers, experiments and commercial indicators rather than activity updates.

Best fit

Useful when the business already has capability but needs stronger coordination.

This can sit above existing agencies, internal teams and suppliers rather than replacing every partner.
