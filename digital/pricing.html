What changes the fee

Scope is driven by complexity and responsibility, not by page count alone.

The starting numbers are there to avoid false low-price anchors. Final investment follows the actual work required.

Depth

Diagnosis versus implementation

A focused decision sprint is different from owning strategy, production, integration and post-launch optimisation.

Dependencies

Technology and third parties

CRM, data, APIs, paid media, specialist design, video, copy or development requirements can materially change scope.

Markets

Single-market versus multi-market

Localisation, channel testing, market-specific landing journeys and operational handoffs increase the work beyond a single-market setup.

Ownership

Project delivery versus ongoing leadership

Ongoing commercial ownership, supplier coordination and optimisation require a different engagement model from a fixed deliverable.

Typical engagement logic

Choose the smallest commitment that can answer the important question.

Diagnostic

Best when the root cause is unclear

Useful before committing to a rebuild, a large media increase or a new CRM programme.

Sprint

Best when a decision needs structure

Useful for positioning, go-to-market, funnel design, measurement architecture or a priority growth problem.

Project

Best when a defined system must be built

Useful for a website, landing system, acquisition setup, CRM workflow or connected implementation.

Partnership

Best when the system needs ongoing ownership

Useful when priorities, optimisation, teams and suppliers need senior direction over time.

Scope the right level

If the fit is unclear, start with the business problem and current constraints.
