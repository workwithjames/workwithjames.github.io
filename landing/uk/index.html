FAQ

Questions to settle before reserving.

Can a British citizen buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreigners and expatriate residents may acquire freehold ownership in Dubai areas designated for foreign ownership, subject to the exact property and registration process.

How are Dubai sale-registration fees structured?

Dubai Land Department’s current sale-registration service lists 2% of the sale value for the buyer and 2% for the seller, plus title deed, map and trustee/service-partner fees. Contractual allocation can differ, so request a dated transaction cost sheet for the exact deal.

Do UK residents pay UK tax on Dubai rental income?

UK residents normally pay UK tax on foreign income, including rental income from overseas property, subject to residence status, circumstances and any applicable relief. Obtain current advice from a qualified UK tax professional.

Can UK residents face UK Capital Gains Tax when selling Dubai property?

Yes, UK residents can be liable to UK Capital Gains Tax on gains from overseas property. Residence status and available reliefs matter, so obtain current professional advice.

Are the GBP figures live FX quotes?

No. The calculator is deliberately editable so you can stress-test the sterling cost. Your bank or FX provider’s executable rate and charges will differ.
