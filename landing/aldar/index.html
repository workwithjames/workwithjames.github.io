FAQ

Questions to settle before reserving

What are Aldar’s current highlighted launches?

Current Aldar opportunities highlighted here include The Canopies at Yas Point, Yas Park Place, Baccarat Residences Saadiyat, Al Ghadeer Gardens and The Wilds Residences. Availability changes by release and is reconfirmed when you enquire.

Which current Aldar release publishes a starting price?

Aldar’s current Al Ghadeer Gardens page advertises a new release from AED 2 million. Prices, unit mix and availability can change, so the exact live release is checked before advice.

Does Aldar only develop in Abu Dhabi?

No. Aldar also has Dubai projects. Location, registration and demand must be assessed by emirate and community.

Can foreign investors buy Aldar property?

Ownership depends on the exact project’s designated investment or freehold status and the applicable emirate process.

Are waterfront homes automatically better investments?

No. View, layout, premium, maintenance, future construction and target demand all matter.
