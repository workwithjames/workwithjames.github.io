Acquisition basis

Underwrite the all-in basis, not the brochure price.

Dubai Land Department currently lists 2% of sale value for the buyer and 2% for the seller on completed-property sale registration, plus applicable title, map and trustee/service-partner charges. Off-plan registration and financing add their own line items.

01DLD registration

Official completed-sale service lists 2% buyer + 2% seller of sale value.

Contractual allocation may differ.
02Transaction extras

Title, map, trustee/service partner, agency, mortgage and professional fees may apply.

Use a dated deal-specific cost sheet.
03Completion capital

Final instalment, snagging, furnishing, utilities, deposits and an operating reserve.

Keep this separate from purchase price.
04Annual operation

Service charges, management, maintenance, vacancy, insurance and future capital work.

These belong in the net-yield model.

US reporting reality

Dubai changes the asset. It does not switch off US rules.

Use qualified US tax advice for your facts. The key distinction is between directly held real estate, foreign financial accounts and interests in foreign entities.

01Worldwide income

IRS guidance says US citizens and resident aliens are generally subject to US tax on worldwide income, including foreign rental income.

Tax treatment depends on your facts.
02Direct real estate

IRS guidance states directly held foreign real estate itself is not reportable on FBAR or Form 8938.

That does not eliminate income-tax reporting.
03Foreign accounts

FBAR can apply when aggregate reportable foreign financial accounts exceed $10,000 at any time in the year.

Property itself is not a financial account.
04Entity ownership

A foreign company, partnership, trust or other structure can create separate US reporting questions.

Choose structure only with tailored advice.

FAQ

Questions to settle before reserving.

Can Americans own Dubai real estate?

Foreign buyers may own eligible property in designated Dubai freehold areas, subject to the exact property, identification and registration process.

Is the UAE dirham fixed to the US dollar?

The Central Bank of the UAE maintains the dirham peg to the US dollar and currently intervenes around USD/AED 3.672–3.673. Bank, card and wire charges can still affect the effective conversion cost.

How are Dubai sale-registration fees structured?

Dubai Land Department currently lists 2% of sale value for the buyer and 2% for the seller on its completed-property sale-registration service, plus applicable title, map and trustee/service-partner fees. Obtain a dated cost sheet for the exact transaction.

Do US taxpayers report Dubai rental income?

US citizens and resident aliens are generally subject to US tax on worldwide income, including foreign rental income. Use a qualified US tax adviser for your circumstances.

Does directly owned Dubai real estate itself go on FBAR or Form 8938?

IRS guidance states directly held foreign real estate itself is not reportable on FBAR or Form 8938. Foreign financial accounts or interests in foreign entities used to hold property may create separate reporting obligations.
