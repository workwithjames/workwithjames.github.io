FAQ

Questions investors ask before reserving

Can Americans own Dubai real estate?

Foreign buyers may own eligible property in designated Dubai freehold areas, subject to title, identification and registration requirements.

Is Dubai rental income tax-free for an American?

Do not assume so. US taxpayers generally need to consider US rules on worldwide income. Obtain current professional advice.

Does owning a Dubai property create an FBAR filing?

Property ownership by itself is not the same as a foreign financial account, but related accounts or structures may create reporting questions. Seek tailored US advice.

Can I finance a Dubai property from the US?

Financing may be available subject to lender criteria, residency, income, valuation and property status. Compare total cost and timing.

Are Dubai rental yields guaranteed?

No. Rent, occupancy, expenses, currency, building performance and future supply can vary.
