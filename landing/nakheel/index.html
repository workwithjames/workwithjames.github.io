FAQ

Questions investors ask before reserving

What are Nakheel’s recent releases?

Nakheel announced another Palm Central phase in June 2026 and a limited Palm Beach Towers collection in May 2026.

What is the stated Palm Jebel Ali villa timing?

Nakheel’s April 2026 contract announcement stated a Q4 2028 target for 544 villas. Review the exact SPA; targets are not guarantees.

Can foreign buyers own at Palm Jebel Ali?

Foreign buyers may own eligible property in designated Dubai freehold areas, subject to exact title and registration requirements.

Is beachfront property guaranteed to appreciate?

No. Supply, delivery, access, maintenance, economic conditions and demand affect value.

How do I verify a water view?

Review the exact orientation, floor, current masterplan, future plots and legal documents rather than relying on a marketing label.
