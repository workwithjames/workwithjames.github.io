FAQ

Questions to settle before reserving

What are Binghatti’s latest highlighted releases?

The official website currently leads with projects including Wraith, Tilal Binghatti and Skyflame. The release list changes frequently.

What is the current Skyflame payment plan?

The official page states 20% on booking, 50% during construction and 30% on completion. Request the dated instalment schedule.

What prices are verified here?

Official current listings show Wraith one-bedroom units from AED 1,299,999, Skyflame one-bedroom units from AED 1,099,999 and Tilal four-bedroom villas from AED 4.2 million. Availability may change.

Is a branded or highly designed project automatically better?

No. Compare the price premium, fees, layout, local supply and likely buyer or tenant demand.

Can I buy remotely?

Remote procedures may be available, but verify identification, signatures, recipient bank details and documents before paying.
